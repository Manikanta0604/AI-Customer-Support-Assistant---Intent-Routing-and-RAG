# Acme Support Knowledge Base

## Refunds
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds return to the original payment method and generally appear within five to ten business days after approval. Digital services already substantially consumed may require manual review.

## Subscription cancellation
A subscription can be cancelled from Settings, Billing, then Manage subscription. Cancellation stops future renewals while access remains available through the current paid period.

## Password reset
Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the email link. Reset links expire after 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check spam and confirm that the entered address matches the account.

## Account security
If an account may be compromised, reset the password immediately, revoke active sessions, and contact human support. Support agents never ask for passwords or full payment-card numbers.

## Delivery
Standard delivery takes three to five business days after shipment. Tracking appears in the order page when the carrier scans the package. Orders without tracking movement for five business days should be escalated.

## Service availability
For service interruptions, check the public status page first. If no incident is listed, capture the time, error message, browser or application version, and steps that reproduce the problem.
